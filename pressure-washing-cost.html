Pressure washing cost guide

How much does pressure washing cost in Hampton Roads?

At Rabbit's Premier Property Care, concrete pressure washing (driveways, walkways, patios and pool decks) is $0.40 per square foot. Wood or composite deck flooring is $0.50 per square foot, a standard 6-foot privacy fence is $3 per linear foot per side, and a house exterior soft wash starts at $300 for one story or $450 for two. A standalone cleaning visit has a $200 minimum, applied once per visit. So a 600 sq. ft. driveway is $240, and a 1,000 sq. ft. driveway is $400. Prices current as of November 2026, for homes in Portsmouth, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Virginia Beach, and Suffolk, Virginia.

Worked examples

What common jobs actually cost.

Each example uses only the published rates above, for one standalone cleaning visit.

Driveway

A 600 sq. ft. concrete driveway at $0.40/sq. ft.: $240. A 1,000 sq. ft. driveway: $400.

Small job, minimum applies

A 300 sq. ft. walkway alone works out to $120, so the $200 visit minimum applies instead.

Driveway, walk and patio together

600 + 150 + 250 sq. ft. of concrete = 1,000 sq. ft. at $0.40: $400 for the visit. One minimum per visit, so combining surfaces costs less than separate trips.

Privacy fence

100 linear feet of 6-foot privacy fence, cleaned on both sides: 200 ft. × $3 = $600.

Deck plus driveway

A 300 sq. ft. deck floor ($150) with a 600 sq. ft. driveway ($240) in one visit: $390.

House soft wash

A one-story house exterior starts at $300; two-story starts at $450. The final price is set at the free estimate.

FAQ

Pressure washing: quick answers

How much does it cost to pressure wash a driveway in Virginia Beach?

At Rabbit's Premier Property Care, concrete driveway cleaning is $0.40 per square foot, with a $200 minimum per standalone visit. A 600 sq. ft. driveway is $240 and a 1,000 sq. ft. driveway is $400. The same rates apply in Portsmouth, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Virginia Beach, and Suffolk.

How much does a house soft wash cost?

A house exterior soft wash starts at $300 for a one-story home and $450 for a two-story home. The final price is confirmed at the free estimate.

Is there a minimum charge?

Yes. A standalone cleaning visit has a $200 minimum. It applies once per visit, not to each surface, so cleaning the driveway, walkway and patio in the same visit shares one minimum.

How much does it cost to clean a fence or a deck?

A standard 6-foot privacy fence is $3 per linear foot, per side. Wood or composite deck flooring is $0.50 per square foot for cleaning; deck railings and stairs are quoted separately.

Do you remove oil, rust, or paint stains?

Oil, rust, and paint treatment is quoted separately from standard cleaning. Ask for it in your free estimate.

Is the estimate free?
