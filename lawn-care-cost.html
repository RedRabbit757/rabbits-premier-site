Lawn care cost guide

How much does lawn care cost in Virginia Beach and Hampton Roads?

At Rabbit's Premier Property Care, a routine lawn visit (mowing, edging, and lawn trimming) is $75 per visit for most average properties. The price depends on property size. If the grass is 6 inches or higher, the visit is 50% more, so a $75 visit becomes $112.50. Shrub and hedge pruning starts at $50, and leaf cleanup starts at $150 per visit. The same prices apply in Virginia Beach, Chesapeake, Norfolk, Suffolk, and Portsmouth, Virginia. Prices current as of November 2026.

FAQ

Lawn care: quick answers

How much does lawn care cost in Virginia Beach?

At Rabbit's Premier Property Care, a routine lawn visit (mowing, edging, and lawn trimming) is $75 per visit for most average properties. The price depends on property size. The same price applies in Chesapeake, Norfolk, Suffolk, and Portsmouth.

What's included in a routine lawn visit?

Mowing, edging, and lawn trimming. Shrub and hedge pruning is not included; it starts at $50, plus $25 to haul away the clippings.

How much more does an overgrown lawn cost?

When the grass is 6 inches or higher, the visit is 50% more than the normal price, so a $75 visit becomes $112.50.

How much does leaf cleanup cost?

Leaf cleanup (raking or blowing and bagging) starts at $150 per visit at Rabbit's Premier Property Care. Hauling leaves and other yard waste away is quoted separately, based on volume.

How much does mulch spreading cost?

Spreading mulch in prepared beds is $75 per cubic yard for labor, with a $150 minimum. Mulch materials and delivery are separate, and bed cleanup is $75 per hour.

What happens if it rains on my service day?

Weather-related rescheduling carries no additional fee. To cancel or reschedule yourself, please give at least 48 hours' notice by calling, texting, or emailing.
