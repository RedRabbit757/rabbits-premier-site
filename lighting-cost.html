Lighting cost guide

How much does Christmas light installation cost in Hampton Roads?

At Rabbit's Premier Property Care, Christmas and holiday light installation labor is $2.83 per linear foot on the first story. Second-story footage is 1.5 times that rate and third-story footage is 2 times. Taking seasonal lights down afterwards is optional and costs 75% of the installation labor. Lights and materials are priced separately, and you're welcome to supply your own. So 100 feet of first-story lights is $283.00 to install, or $495.25 with removal at the end of the season. Prices current as of November 2026, for homes in Portsmouth, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Virginia Beach, and Suffolk, Virginia.

FAQ

Christmas light installation: quick answers

How much does Christmas light installation cost in Virginia Beach?

At Rabbit's Premier Property Care, installation labor is $2.83 per linear foot on the first story, 1.5 times that on the second story and 2 times on the third. 100 feet of first-story lights is $283.00 in labor. Lights and materials are separate. The same rates apply in Portsmouth, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Virginia Beach, and Suffolk.

How much does it cost to take Christmas lights down?

Seasonal removal is optional and costs 75% of the installation labor total, not including lights or materials. For example, 100 feet of first-story lights is $283.00 to install and $212.25 to remove.

Can I supply my own lights?

Yes. Rabbit's Premier Property Care installs customer-provided lights for both seasonal and permanent installations. Billy can also recommend lights. Lights and materials are always priced separately from installation labor.

Do you install permanent outdoor lighting?

Yes. Permanent outdoor lighting is installed at the same labor rates as seasonal displays, and permanent installations stay up, so there is no removal charge.

How is second- and third-story lighting priced?

Each story above the first adds 50% of the $2.83 base rate, applied only to the footage on that story: $4.245 per foot on the second story and $5.66 per foot on the third. First-story footage stays at $2.83 per linear foot on any house.

Is the estimate free?

Yes. Request one by phone or text at 757-765-8212, by email, or with the quote form. Height and access limits are confirmed during the free estimate.
