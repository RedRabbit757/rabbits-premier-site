FAQ

Quick answers

What's included in a routine lawn visit?

Mowing, edging, and lawn trimming. Most average properties are $75 per visit; the price depends on property size. Shrub and hedge pruning is not included.

When does the overgrown-lawn charge apply?

When the grass is 6 inches or higher. Overgrown lawns are 50% more than the normal visit price, so a $75 visit becomes $112.50.

Is hauling away clippings included with pruning?

Clipping haul-away is $25 on top of pruning, which starts at $50, so pruning with haul-away starts at $75. The $25 fee covers pruning clippings only; leaf and other yard-waste hauling is quoted separately by volume.

Can I supply my own lights?

Yes. Customer-provided lights are accepted for seasonal and permanent installations. Billy can also recommend lights. Lights and materials are always priced separately from installation labor.

How is second- and third-story lighting priced?

Installation labor is $2.83 per linear foot at the base rate. Each story above the first adds 50% of the base rate, applied only to the footage installed on that story — second-story footage is 1.5 times the base rate, third-story footage is 2 times. First-story footage stays at the base rate.

Do you take seasonal lights down?

Yes, on request. Seasonal removal is 75% of the installation labor total, not including lights or materials. For example, 100 feet of first-story lights is $283.00 to install and $212.25 to remove.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes — small commercial properties and property managers are welcome. Commercial and property-management work requires an on-site assessment and a custom quote; the published prices apply to residential work.

Are estimates free?

Yes. Request one by phone, text, or email, or use the quote form.

Do you require a deposit?

Jobs over $1,000 require a 25% deposit, applied toward the total. The remaining balance is due on completion. See our policies for cancellation and rescheduling details.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, card, and check.
