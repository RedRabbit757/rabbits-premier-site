Policies

Plain-language policies so you know what to expect before work begins.

Deposits, Cancellations & Rescheduling

Jobs over $1,000 require a 25% deposit, applied toward the total. The remaining balance is due upon completion.

Please provide at least 48 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule by calling, texting, or emailing us.

If you cancel, your deposit will be refunded minus documented costs for work already authorized and completed, and any nonreturnable materials purchased specifically for your project with your approval. Any deductions will be itemized.

Weather-related rescheduling carries no additional fee. If we cancel before work begins and cannot arrange a suitable new date, your deposit will be refunded in full.

This policy does not limit any cancellation or refund rights provided by law.

Our Satisfaction Promise

We take pride in our work. If something in the agreed scope hasn't been completed properly, please contact us within seven days of completion. We'll review your concern and, if it results from our workmanship, correct it at no additional labor or material cost.

This promise covers the work we agreed to perform. It does not cover additional services, pre-existing damage, normal wear, weather damage, or defects in customer-supplied materials. Our installation workmanship remains covered when you supply the materials.

The seven-day reporting window does not limit any rights or warranties provided by law.

Estimates & Payment

Estimates are free. We accept cash, Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, card, and check.
